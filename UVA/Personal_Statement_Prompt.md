Personal Statement

Our PhD program uses a holistic admissions process and written statements are an important way for the applicant to provide information that can contribute to a holistic review. In addition to describing your motivations for pursuing the PhD in CS we ask that your statement explicitly address these prompts:

1) Describe the themes that summarize your general research interests and how they relate to our program. To understand our research interests, look through the web-sites and recently published papers of UVA CS faculty members marked as "Currently Recruiting". If these themes align with your prior research experience, explain why you want to continue with them. If the themes are different, explain your rationale for changing your focus to align with our program.

Currently Recruiting faculty: https://engineering.virginia.edu/department/computer-science/faculty

2) What do you believe provides the strongest evidence for your readiness to pursue graduate studies? Speculate on how you think your readiness might align with a specific advising or mentorship style: hands-on vs. hands-off, individual vs. team-based, etc.

Individual documents should not exceed 25 pages
